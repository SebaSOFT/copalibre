# Translation review ledger

Record human reviews of interface copy here. A review entry names its reviewer, date and the
catalogue areas covered; list unresolved wording issues so a later contributor can continue the
review. Review status is `unreviewed`, `partially reviewed` or `reviewed`.

The catalogue areas are control, public, TV, emails and help. Do not mark an area reviewed because
automated completeness or copy checks pass; those checks do not replace a fluent reader.
