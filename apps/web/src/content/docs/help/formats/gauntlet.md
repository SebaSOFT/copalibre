## Overview

The Gauntlet (also known as a Stepladder bracket) is an ascending knockout ladder. Rather than a symmetric elimination tree, competitors challenge upwards one round at a time: the lowest-seeded qualifiers battle first, and each match winner climbs to face the next higher seed.

## Bracket Mechanics

- **Round 1**: Seed N challenges Seed N-1.
- **Round 2**: Winner of Round 1 challenges Seed N-2.
- **Subsequent Rounds**: Progression continues match by match up the ladder.
- **Grand Final**: The surviving challenger plays Seed 1 for the championship.

## Strategic Purpose

The Gauntlet rewards regular-season excellence: higher-seeded entrants enjoy bye protection into late stages, requiring fewer postseason wins to claim the title.
