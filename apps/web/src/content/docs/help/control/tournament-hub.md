## What this screen is for

Lists every stage the tournament declares — its number, name, format, and whether it has been
seeded yet. Selecting a stage opens that stage's own hub, where its identity, its seeding, and its
zones, standings and schedule tools all live.

## Key fields

- **Stage**: one phase of the tournament (for example, "Group stage" or "Playoffs") — a tournament
  can declare several, in order.
- **Seeded**: whether the stage already holds a generated fixture. An unseeded stage can still be
  renamed, reformatted or removed; a seeded one can only be renamed.

## Reaching this screen

Open it from the "Stages" action on a tournament's card on the organization dashboard, or by
navigating directly to a tournament's own control URL.
